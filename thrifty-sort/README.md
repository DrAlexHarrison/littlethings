# Thrifty Sort

Walmart and Amazon search results in order of price per ounce, per fluid ounce or per count, cheapest first.

- The sorts are in each store's own Sort menu, listed first.
- The first three pages of results are sorted as one list.
- It turns on by itself for groceries and household staples. On other searches, pick it in the Sort menu.
- On Walmart, a Walmart Cash offer counts as money off, and the item says so.
- Sponsored items, repeats and out-of-stock items are left out while one of these sorts is on.

## Install

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera:

1. On [github.com/DrAlexHarrison/littlethings](https://github.com/DrAlexHarrison/littlethings), click Code, then Download ZIP, and unzip it.
2. Keep the `thrifty-sort/extension` folder somewhere it can stay.
3. Open `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick that folder.
4. Reload your Walmart and Amazon tabs.

Firefox: open [thrifty-sort-1.8.1.xpi](https://github.com/DrAlexHarrison/littlethings/releases/download/thrifty-sort-v1.8.1/thrifty-sort-1.8.1.xpi), then Continue to Installation, then Add.

Or ask your AI to "install Thrifty Sort from github.com/DrAlexHarrison/littlethings".
